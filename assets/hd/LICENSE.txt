HD people (m_mundu, m_pants, m_tee, f_saree, f_churidar, f_jeans .glb)

These bodies were built with Ara 3D Gen (https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen,
MIT licence), which says: "Characters exported by Ara 3D Gen may be used in
commercial and non-commercial projects without attribution."

The body meshes, skins, eyes, hair and clothes come from MakeHuman
(http://www.makehumancommunity.org), released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain) in September 2020 by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and
Jonas Hauquier: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No motion capture is included: the game poses these bodies with its own
animation rig (js/hd.js).

Textures were resized to 1024 px and stored as WebP with gltf-transform.
